eFS Ticket Downloader · Privacy policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Applies to version 1.1.0 and later

In short

eFS Ticket Downloader downloads the documents in a court case file on the Malaysian Courts e-Filing System (eFS) ticket search and names them consistently. It does its work inside your browser. It does not have its own server, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not sell data.

Stays in your browser

  • Ticket and case numbers you enter
  • Case details and document lists read from eFS
  • Which documents you have downloaded
  • The documents themselves

Shared with ExtensionPay

  • An anonymous ID for your install
  • Your email, only if you pay or log in
  • Payment details, entered on Stripe's checkout, never seen by the extension

1Who this policy covers

This policy covers the eFS Ticket Downloader extension for Google Chrome and other Chromium browsers ("the extension"), made by an independent developer ("we"). The extension is not made, endorsed or supported by the Federal Court of Malaysia, the Office of the Chief Registrar or the e-Kehakiman team. Your use of eFS itself is governed by eFS's own terms and policies.

2What the extension handles

DataWhyWhere it goes
Ticket number and case number you type into the extension To fill in and run the eFS ticket search for you, and to remember them so you don't need to retype them Your browser and eFS, as part of the search you asked for
Case details shown on the eFS case page: case number, the first-named party on each side, and the document list (enclosure number, document type, filing date) To show the download panel and to name the case folder and files Your browser only
Download history: which enclosures you have saved for each case For the "Skip already downloaded" option Your browser only
Number of free downloads used for each case To apply the free allowance Your browser only
Licence status (paid or not) and, for paying users, the email used to pay To unlock unlimited downloads and show which account is active ExtensionPay and a copy in your browser
An anonymous identifier created for your install So ExtensionPay can link your install to a payment ExtensionPay

The extension does not read or store identity card or passport numbers shown on eFS, the contents of the documents you download, your browsing history, or anything on websites other than eFS and ExtensionPay.

3How the extension uses this data

Only to provide its single purpose: searching an eFS ticket, listing the case documents, downloading the ones you choose with clear file names, and managing the free and paid versions. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling, credit or lending decisions, or anything unrelated to that purpose.

The use of this data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4Who receives data

  • eFS (efs.kehakiman.gov.my). The extension talks to eFS on your behalf, using your own ticket session, in the same way you would by clicking through the site.
  • ExtensionPay and Stripe. Payments for the paid version are handled by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which uses Stripe to process cards. ExtensionPay receives the anonymous install ID and, if you pay or log in, your email. Card details are entered on Stripe's checkout page and never pass through the extension. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies, available on their websites.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone else, and the extension sends no ticket numbers, case details or documents to ExtensionPay, Stripe or us.

5Storage and how long it is kept

Data kept by the extension is stored with Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. Downloaded documents are saved to your Downloads folder like any other download.

  • Reset history in the download panel clears the download history for that case.
  • Removing the extension deletes everything it has stored in your browser. Files you have downloaded stay in your Downloads folder until you delete them.
  • ExtensionPay keeps payment records for as long as it needs them to provide the paid version and meet its legal obligations.

6Permissions the extension asks for

  • Downloads, to save the documents you choose.
  • Storage, to keep the items listed in section 2 on your device.
  • Access to efs.kehakiman.gov.my, to run the ticket search and read the case page's document list.
  • Access to extensionpay.com, to confirm a completed payment so the extension can unlock straight away.

7Your choices and rights

You can clear the download history at any time, or remove the extension to delete everything it stores. If you have paid, you can ask us to access, correct or delete the email and licence record held with ExtensionPay, including under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010. Email us at rachelcjw101@gmail.com.

8Children

The extension is a tool for people who use the court e-Filing System, such as lawyers, litigants and their staff. It is not directed at children.

9Changes to this policy

If the extension starts handling data differently, we will update this page and change the effective date above before releasing that version.
