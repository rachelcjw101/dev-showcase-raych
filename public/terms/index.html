  • We are not responsible for a missed, late or wrongly attended hearing, or any costs, orders or consequences that follow.
  • Calendar services you add entries to (such as Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar) are governed by their own terms.

18Stamp Duty Calculator

  • The calculator gives an estimate based on the Stamp Act 1949 and the remission and exemption orders it reflects, as at the "rates last checked" date shown on the page. Later changes to the law may not be included.
  • Stamp duty is assessed by the Collector of Stamp Duties through LHDN. The assessed amount is the one that counts, and it may differ from the estimate, for example because of the instrument's wording, valuation, or remissions and exemptions that need an application.
  • Check the figure, and the time limit for stamping, before you advise a client, quote a figure or submit an instrument. We are not responsible for under- or over-payment, late-stamping penalties or other consequences of relying on the estimate.
